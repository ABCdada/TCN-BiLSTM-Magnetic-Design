Grid-side converter window

Converter and magnetic-model demonstration

Configure a deterministic grid-side converter window, reconstruct the induced magnetic trajectory, and pass it through the selected TCN-BiLSTM material model.

Scope of this demonstration. The converter voltage and nominal current are calculated from deterministic converter state equations and the AI model is then used for magnetic post-processing. The AI prediction is not fed back into every converter time step, and this page is not an experimental validation. The effective-area and magnetic-path-length conversion is appropriate only for an approximately uniform toroidal core or equivalent magnetic circuit.
